Our Story

Ubuntu Tech Solutions was founded in 2021 by two passionate IT graduates who met while studying at the Durban University of Technology. Frustrated by the lack of affordable, honest technical support available in small businesses and schools in Durban, they decided to build a company rooted in the African philosophy of ubuntu - "I am because we are".

What began as a two-person operation fixing laptops in the garage has grown into a trusted IT partner for over 50 local organizations. We believe technology should bring people together and empower communities, not create barriers. This principle guides client relationship we build.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and ethical IT support that empowers small businesses across Durban.

Our Vision

To become Durban's most trusted IT partner, recognized for our technical excellence, community impact, and unwavering integrity in every interaction.
